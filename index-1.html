I'm a frontend engineer and UI/UX designer based in Jakarta, Indonesia, with four years of experience turning ambitious design ideas into fast, accessible, and delightful web experiences. My work sits at the intersection of design and engineering — I care as much about how an interface feels to use as I do about how clean the code behind it is.

Over the years I've helped startups and design studios ship dashboards, marketing sites, and product interfaces that balance visual polish with real-world performance. I'm especially drawn to motion design, glassmorphism, and building component systems that scale.
